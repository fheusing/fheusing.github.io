Felix von Heusinger

I am a PhD student in Sociology at DIW Berlin and affiliated with the BGSS at HU Berlin. In my thesis, I explore how the intergenerational transmission of homeownership is changing. Together with Sabine Zinn, I am developing and expanding the MicSim R package, which I use to build microsimulations in which parental attributes directly shape children's life outcomes.

Research Interests

I am interested in how we can model complex interactions between individuals when data is scarce, and in how access to wealth shifts between family generations. In particular, I study how demographic and economic change interact across generations to reproduce or disrupt social inequalities.
